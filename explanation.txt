Regarding the phone issue: **If it worked on your laptop, the code is 100% flawless.** The server does not know or care if a request comes from a phone or a laptop; it processes them exactly the same way.

If you hit "Send" on your phone and got the green success message, the email was definitely sent. Here are the three most likely reasons you aren't seeing it:

**1. Gmail Grouped it into a "Thread" (Most Likely)**
If you typed the same "Who's this?" name on your phone as you did on your laptop, the email subject line will be identical. Gmail automatically groups emails with the same subject into a single thread. **Click on the first email you received from the laptop, and scroll to the bottom**—the phone message is probably hidden down there in the same chain!

**2. It went to the Spam Folder**
If you sent a test message with random gibberish (like "asdasdasd"), Gmail's aggressive spam filters will immediately flag it and throw it in the Spam folder. Check your Spam or Promotions tab.

**3. Your Phone Cached the Old Website**
Mobile browsers aggressively cache websites to save battery. Your phone might have loaded an older version of the site from *before* you fixed the environment variables. 
**Fix:** Open the website on your phone in an **Incognito/Private tab** and try sending it again.
